FAQ

Common questions.

What is a larp tool?

A larp tool lets you visually roleplay a setup you don't actually have — spoofed Robux, Premium, Verified, limiteds, and so on. Everything is cosmetic and local. Nothing about your real account is changed.

How does the extension work?

Aegis injects into the Roblox website and modifies what you see locally. It changes profile data, avatar renders, currency displays, and more — all on your end. Other people see your real profile; you see the spoofed version.

Is Aegis safe to use?

Yes. Aegis runs entirely in your browser and never communicates with Roblox servers. It doesn't require login credentials or account access, and every change is purely visual.

Can this get my account banned?

There's nothing for Roblox to detect since Aegis has no server-side footprint. That said, don't use it to scam people or misrepresent content in bad faith — that's on you.

Does in-game sync actually work?

Yes. The sync module bridges the gap between the website spoof and the game client, so your avatar and identity changes show up in-game too. You need to run Aegis BootStrapper.bat from the Roblox home screen in the app for it to work.

Why is Aegis free?

Aegis is a passion project. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no ads.

Can I request features?

Yes. Feature suggestions are welcome in the Discord. Requests that fit the tool's scope get prioritized based on demand.
